Fast aircraft and drones. A collision, an engine failure, a flash that blinds every camera. When the view is lost, what should the screen say?

MoVi declares what it has not observed. Three answers per track. I see it. I don't see it, but here is where it can be. Or: I don't know.

An aircraft on approach, and a drone that broadcasts nothing. In six flight scenarios, MoVi never said clear where an aircraft, a drone or a fragment was.

An engine fails at a hundred and thirty-five metres per second. A flash, then smoke. MoVi infers the event and declares both cameras unreliable within three cycles, in sixty of sixty simulated runs. Low-energy debris stays inside the declared volume in fifty-eight of sixty.

Two drones close at forty and thirty-five metres per second. MoVi warns of the close pass at least ten seconds ahead. After the impact, the collision is not yet recognised as an event, but the region is kept: in every simulated run, the fragments stayed inside it.

A flash blinds every optical sensor for three seconds. Radar alone keeps a region for every track.

Now continuity is lost. In the motion model, the confidence is computed exactly, in closed form. A fixed zone loses it in seconds, so the region grows to keep nine chances in ten. On sixteen real aircraft tracks, with a simulated sensor, it held them ninety-two to ninety-eight times in a hundred, from three to thirty seconds. At one to two seconds, only seventy-one: a declared limit.

Dedicated hardware? Chips built for one job, like those now serving language models, suggest processing in about a millisecond: an estimate by analogy. The sensor's revisit still dominates the delay.

The same core works on a site: a drone behind a building, warned in time in two hundred of two hundred runs. And at short range: people, cars, trains.

The limits. Everything is simulated, with no physical sensor, and classes are labelled by hand. From prototype to deployed system: a contact rule for fast collisions, state-of-the-art sensors, dedicated hardware, field trials. MoVi. It observes and warns. By SDMs.
