An apron. A hangar. Two inspection drones. An aircraft taxiing. One drone slips behind the hangar. What should the screen say?

MoVi gives every tracked object one of three answers. I see it. I don't see it: here is where it can be. Or: I don't know. What it has not observed, it marks unverified, never empty. A possible-presence warning stays on until a sensor looks again.

Radar, LiDAR, visible and thermal cameras, and what aircraft and drones broadcast. All simulated from public data sheets. Each sensor has blind spots, and MoVi draws them.

Fog, then heavy rain. By calculation, the cameras' reach collapses, and MoVi declares it, per sensor. Only the radar is left: no redundancy. If the radar also fails, a fault we inject, reliability is lost: warnings would come too late.

Two drones fly close. Too close to separate: unresolved tracks. Event: contact, inferred. A possible-debris volume opens, and never shrinks on its own. {{E1-X}}

An engine fails during taxi, with a flash. {{E2-C}} In the model, the radar alone still sees the aircraft. Behind the smoke: not observed.
