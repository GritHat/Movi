MoVi declares what it has not observed.
For every object, one of three answers. I see it. I don't see it, and here is where it can be. Or: I don't know.
What it has not observed, it never calls empty. A possible-presence warning stays on until a sensor looks again.

Radar, LiDAR, visible and thermal cameras, and what aircraft and drones broadcast. All simulated from public data sheets.
Each sensor has blind spots, and MoVi draws them. A silent transmitter proves nothing.

Fog rolls in.
The cameras' useful range collapses, and MoVi says so, per sensor, per class. Only the radar is left: no redundancy.
If the radar drops too: reliability lost, warning not timely.

The fog lifts. Two drones fly close.

A volume where debris can be, which never shrinks on its own, and a warning to the person below. The ground stays unverified until inspected.
